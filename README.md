# Kathe Todd-Brown

I'm a computational biogeochemist who makes computer models of how soil breaths and links up data to support those models.

I am an Associate Professor at the University of Florida in the Environmental Engineering Department where I teach courses on systems modeling, scientific writing, and climate science.
I have previously been a Post-doctoral Fellow at University of Oklahoma (Norman, OK), Pacific Northwest National Laboratory (Richland, WA), and Wilfrid Laurier University (Waterloo, Ontario).
I hold a PhD (2013) from the Earth System Science Department of University of California Irvine and a BS (2004) in Mathematics from Harvey Mudd College in Claremont, CA.
